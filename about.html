About

Our Story

The Waveline Apparel began to make waves among other locally-owned apparels in the year 2023. What started as a small group project among students, has now turned into one of the most popular clothing stores in Terengganu, Malaysia. It was here in Chendering, Kuala Terengganu where Waveline opened its first store, offering modern, minimalistic and classy clothes to their customers. The name of the store was inspired by the calming beaches here in Terengganu. We hope to invoke the sense of “coolness” that the waves here in Terengganu are able to give in the apparel we make.

Waveline Apparel is a retail apparel store selling products that are suitable for customers of various ages and sizes. The product line is categorized into four main segments: women, men, children, and accessories. For the men and women segments, Waveline Apparel offers graphical t-shirts and sweatshirts. For the children's segment, the clothing is sold as ready-made outfits to ensure ease of wear. The children's clothes are to be worn from the ages 4 to 7 years old. Lastly, Waveline Apparel offers a myriad of accessories. From sunglasses, earrings to tote bags, Waveline Apparel sells everything their customers might need to look classy and cool.

Waveline Apparel has one goal in mind, which was to make clothes that were both classy, modern and still up to taste to the younger generation. We in Waveline Apparel believe that clothes should be something that is timeless but ever-changing. This is to show that Waveline Apparel can be worn by everyone both young and old while still retaining its signature style. For instance, Waveline Apparel offers sizes for men and women from size super small(XS) to double large(XXL).
