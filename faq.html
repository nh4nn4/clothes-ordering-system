Frequently Asked Questions

Have any questions? We got you!

My Account
How do I update my personal information?

To update your personal information:

  1. 1. Log in to your account
  2. 2. Click on "Account Settings" in the top right menu
  3. 3. Edit any of your personal details
  4. 4. Click "Save Changes" to update your information

Note: Some information like your email address may require verification before changes are applied.

Orders
How can I track my order?

You can track your order in several ways:

  • - Use the tracking number sent to your email
  • - Log in to your account and view "My Orders"
  • - Contact our customer service with your order number

Most orders are processed within 24 hours and shipping updates are provided every step of the way.

How long does shipping take?

Shipping times vary depending on your location:

  • - Domestic orders: 2-5 business days
  • - International orders: 7-14 business days
  • - Express shipping: 1-3 business days (additional fee applies)

During peak seasons, please allow additional processing time.

Return and refunds
What do I do if an item from my delivery is missing or defective?

Please contact us for assistance via email (WavelineAppSupport@gmail.com). Any damage or discrepancy must be reported to Waveline Apparel within 24 hours after receiving the order. We would require you to submit a clear photo of the damaged parts or packaging of the parcel to us within 24 hours in order to expedite the claims process. You will be required to return the damaged or defective items to the nearest courier branch using the Return Shipping Form provided.

How soon will I receive my refund?

Refunds will be done via initial payment mode and may take up to 14 working days. Please note that refund amount is subject to the conditions of the products.

Payments
What payment methods can I use?

Our websites accept credit cards and debit cards. However, fear not, our cashier in store accepts cash as well.

Coupons
How to use coupon?

You can use your coupon by doing these steps:

  1. 1. Proceed to your shopping cart
  2. 2. Enter your coupon code to apply the coupon.
  3. 3. Click on 'Apply Coupon' button before you proceed to checkout
  4. 4. Now, you have successfully redeem the coupon.
Product Safety
Are your products tested for safety?

Yes, all our products undergo rigorous safety testing.
